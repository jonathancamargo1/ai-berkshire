# Industry Funnel Screening: Value Investment Selection Process from 30-60 to 3 Companies

Execute funnel-style value investment screening for $ARGUMENTS industry/direction, from full market scan to 3 final picks.

## Applicable Scenarios

When you mention an industry or investment direction (like "AI Computing Power", "Innovative Pharmaceuticals", "Robotics"), wanting to:
1. Not miss any important target (including A-shares, Hong Kong, US, unlisted candidates)
2. Use unified standards to filter out "story stocks" and substandard companies
3. Concentrate energy on the truly top 3 worth deep research
4. Each layer has clear, traceable, reviewable criteria

Difference with `industry-research`:
- `industry-research` emphasizes industry chain structure and big picture
- `industry-funnel` emphasizes stock selection filtering, refining from full market to 3

The two can complement: first use `industry-research` to understand industry structure, then use `industry-funnel` to select targets.

---

## Funnel Structure Overview

```
Layer 1: Full Market Scan               30-60 companies   (activity+momentum+top 30 market cap)
        ↓ 5 Hard Value Investing Criteria
Layer 2: Rough Screening                ≤ 10 companies   (5 pass + moat ★★★+)
        ↓ Detailed Analysis
Layer 3: Detailed Analysis              ≤ 10 companies  (each 300-500 chars structured analysis)
        ↓ Final Selection
Layer 4: Four Masters Deep Analysis     3 companies     (each 800-1200 chars, 4 perspectives)
        ↓
Output: Investment Recommendation + Operating Signal + Position Sizing
```

Each layer's "filtered companies" must leave elimination reason record, cannot be black box.

## First Step: Full Market Scan

### 1.1 Active Stock Definition (Three Categories Combined)

**Category A - Trading Activity**:
- Top 30 in average daily volume last 30 days (A/HK/US each)

**Category B - Momentum Ranking**:
- Top 20 in momentum last 30 days
- Top 20 in momentum last 90 days
- Union of both

**Category C - Market Cap Anchor**:
- Top 30 in market cap in industry (regardless of up/down)

Final combination = A ∪ B ∪ C, expected 30-60 companies.

### 1.2 Markets Must Search

| Market | Source Suggestion |
|------|----------|
| A-Shares (Shanghai/Shenzhen) | Tonghuashun/Eastmoney Industry Panel, Thinkorswim |
| Hong Kong | Futu/Tonghuashun HK, HKEX Industry Classification |
| US | NASDAQ/NYSE Industry ETF Holdings, Yahoo Finance |
| International Markets | Japan/Korea/Taiwan/Europe companies cannot be missed (especially semiconductors, electronics) |
| Unlisted Companies | Separate section "Future IPO Candidates", note valuation and IPO timing |

### 1.3 Output Format

| Company Name | Code | Market | Market Cap | Business One-Liner | Industry % | Category (A/B/C) |
|-------|------|-----|------|----------|-----------|--------|

**Key Self-Check**:
- Industry % < 30%, be cautious with "tangential stocks", mark "non-pure target"
- China/Asia market, don't miss due to lack of English data
- Small market cap, don't miss due to AI favoring large cap

## Second Step: 5 Hard Value Investing Criteria for Rough Screening → ≤ 10 Companies

For the 30-60 companies from step one, apply 5 criteria to each.

### 2.1 5 Hard Criteria

| # | Criterion | Pass Standard | Relaxation Condition | Data Source |
|---|------|---------|---------|----------|
| 1 | PE Valuation | Reasonable (compare with historical range, industry) | High growth relaxed to PEG < 1.5 | Financial Report+Wind/Tonghuashun |
| 2 | ROE | > 15% or 3-year trend improving | Capital-intensive industry can relax | Financial Report |
| 3 | Operating Cash Flow | Positive and >70% of net profit | — | Financial Report |
| 4 | Debt Ratio | < 60% | Utilities/Energy can relax to 70% | Financial Report |
| 5 | Moat Quick Assessment | ★★★+ | — | Qualitative Judgment |

**5 Types of Moat**:
- Brand/Pricing Power
- Switching Cost/User Stickiness
- Network Effect
- Scale Effect
- Technical/License/Resource Barrier

### 2.2 Output Format

| Company | PE | ROE | Op CF/Net Profit | Debt Ratio | Moat | Combined | Keep/Eliminate | Elimination Reason |
|------|----|----|-----------|--------|-------|------|------|--------|

**Retention Rules**:
- 5 pass → Keep directly
- 4 pass + 1 borderline → Keep with yellow flag
- Fewer than 4 → Eliminate, note reason

**Target**: Keep ≤ 10 companies. If keeping too many (> 12), raise moat bar to ★★★★ and screen again.

**Valuation Quick Take**:
- Current PE/PS/EV/EBITDA + Position in historical range

### 3.2 Final 3 Selection Criteria

Not by ranking, but by "Portfolio Complementarity":
- At least 1 "High Certainty Low Volatility"（Buffett Type）
- At least 1 "Medium Certainty Medium Volatility"（Growth Type）
- Optionally 1 "High Volatility High Risk"（Option Type）

If can't find 3 good enough in any sub-track, better write "2 Final + 1 Watch" than force 3.

## Fourth Step: Four Masters Deep Analysis（3 Companies, each 800-1200 chars）

Execute four masters perspective deep analysis on final 3.

### 4.1 Duan Yongping Perspective: Business Essence

- Define in one sentence what business this company does
- Is it a good business? Why?
- What is the "essence" of this business? Has management strayed?
- Where is the "sustainability" of the business model?

### 4.2 Buffett Perspective: Moat Depth

- Rate five moat types（★1-5）, list specific evidence
- Will this moat exist in 10 years?
- Where is the "margin of safety" for buying now?

|-------|------|--------|

### 4.3 Munger Perspective: Risk and Failure Mode

- How will this company most likely fail?（List 3 failure paths）
- How much is it worth in worst case?（Extreme scenario valuation）

## AI Research Bias Self-Awareness（Important）

Funnel screening process where AI easily trips:

| Bias | Manifestation | Response |
|------|-----|------|
| Large Cap Preference | Big company more data, longer analysis, seems "better" | Rate by hard criteria and moat, not report length |
| English Preference | Abundant US data, A/HK easy to underestimate | Must search both Chinese/English, don't miss A/H company |
| Story Preference | High momentum + Media heat = Better "AI concept stock" | Distinguish "% AI revenue" vs "% AI story", see real business |
| Current Preference | Currently good financial company easy to select, miss turnaround dark horse | Layer 2 screening allows "trend improving" as relaxation |
| Listed Preference | Only listed companies miss best player in track | Must list "Future IPO Candidate", note valuation and timing |

## Output Requirements

1. **Report Location**: `reports/{Industry Name}-funnel-{YYYYMMDD}.md`（Industry report place in reports/ root）
2. **Language**: Chinese
3. **Style**: Direct, sharp, no bullshit
4. **Data**: All data note source; estimated values mark "estimated"
5. **Don't Pre-Set Stance**: First data → Then logic → Then conclusion. Conclusion should emerge naturally from data
6. **Both Sides**: Every major judgment attach counter-argument（"But on the other hand..."）, let reader weigh
7. **Leave Elimination Record for Each Layer**: Eliminated company also leave name+reason
