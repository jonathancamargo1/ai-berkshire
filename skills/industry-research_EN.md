# Industry Investment Research: Complete Supply Chain Scan + Four Masters Analysis Framework

Conduct systematic industry supply chain investment research on $ARGUMENTS.

## Research Objective

Starting from an investment theme/logic, complete:
1. Validate each link of the investment logic chain
2. Draw the complete industry supply chain landscape map
3. Scan all global listed companies (A-share/Hong Kong/US/International)
4. For each supply chain segment, execute four masters framework analysis on leading companies
5. Output: industry-level portfolio configuration recommendation

---

## First Step: Investment Logic Chain Construction and Validation

### 1.1 Draw the logic chain
Use arrows to express causal relationship from "fundamental trend" to "beneficiaries".

### 1.2 Validate each link
For each arrow in logic chain, question and seek evidence.

### 1.3 Find "already-occurred validation events"
List real commercial events already signed/implemented (rather than forecasts).

---

## Second Step: Supply Chain Landscape Map Drawing

### 2.1 Draw supply chain structure
Decompose industry into upstream→midstream→downstream→auxiliary segments.

### 2.2 Identify "business characteristics" of each segment
Mark: business model, gross margin, competitive format, barrier type, cyclicality.

### 2.3 Mark "bottleneck segments"
Identify where supply is tightest, substitution is hardest, profit margin is highest.

---

## AI Research Bias Self-Awareness: Special Pitfalls of Industry Research

Industry research has unique ways AI data bias amplifies.

**Industry-Level Biases**: Mature industry preference, emerging industry underestimation, market leader preference, listed company preference, English language preference.

**Anti-Bias Measures**: Not only list "companies AI easily finds", actively search for obscure targets. For small companies, don't lower grade just because analysis is brief. Mark "information sufficiency" A/B/C in final report.

---

## Third Step: Global Listed Company Scanning

Use Task tool to launch background Agent for comprehensive search.

### Search Checklist
- US companies, A-share, Hong Kong, other international markets
- Industry ETFs
- Key unlisted companies

### For each company collect
- Name, stock code, exchange
- Market cap (approximate)
- One-sentence description
- Whether pure play or diversified
- Supply chain segment

### Output Format
Classify by segment, stratify by investment certainty: Tier 1 (large-cap/pure/leader), Tier 2 (mid-cap/pure/sub-leader), Tier 3 (small-cap/development), Tier 4 (diversified).

---

## Fourth Step: Four Masters Analysis of Leading Companies in Each Segment

For Tier 1 and Tier 2 companies of each supply chain segment, execute analysis below.

### 4.1 Business Essence (Duan Yongping)
- One-sentence definition, revenue structure, margins, cash flow
- Is this a good business?

### 4.2 Moat (Buffett)
Moat strength rating in five types (★1-5): brand, switching costs, network, scale, technology.

### 4.3 Risk (Munger)
- How likely to fail? Worst case value? Why don't smart people buy?

### 4.4 Management (Duan + Buffett)
- Who is CEO? Key decision history. Ownership. Brief evaluation A/B/C.

### 4.5 Valuation
- Current PE/PS/EV/EBITDA, comparison with peers, brief evaluation

### 4.6 Recommendation Degree
★★★★★ = core position, ★★★★☆ = satellite, ★★★☆☆ = watch, ★★☆☆☆ = high-risk, ★☆☆☆☆ = not recommended

## Fifth Step: Industry-Level Risk Assessment

### 5.1 Systematic Risk Checklist
One link refuted, substitute technology, policy black swan, demand cycles down, valuation bubble.

### 5.2 Historical Analogy
Find similar theme in history, analyze final outcome: who won? Did investors make money?

### 5.3 Bias Self-Check
Story too perfect? Anchored by recent gains? Herd effect?
