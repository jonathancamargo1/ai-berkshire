# Private Company Research: Multi-Agent Deep Research Framework

Perform team-based deep research analysis for `$ARGUMENTS`. Designed especially for private companies like Ant Group, Little Red Book, SpaceX, Stripe.

**Final Goal**: Under conditions of naturally scarce information, restore as much as possible the **true value** of this company — not the valuation the market gives, but what the business itself is worth.

## Framework Characteristics

Core difference of private company vs public company research:
- **No standardized financial reports**: Needs multiple source collage, cross-verification
- **Few valuation anchoring points**: Depends on funding rounds, comparable company method, scenario analysis
- **Large information asymmetry**: Needs more 'puzzle assembly' research method
- **Uncertain exit path**: IPO/acquisition/secondary transfer all possible
