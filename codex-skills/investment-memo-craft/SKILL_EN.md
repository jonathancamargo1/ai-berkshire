## Purpose

Transform investment research into decision-ready Codex report. Keep the data discipline of the underlying research skill, but make the output easier for an investor to use: concrete business mechanics, sharp inverse thinking, explicit opportunity cost, action thresholds and calm Markdown typography.

Use this as writing and judgment overlay. Does not replace financial data rules, primary source checks, valuation tools or report audit tooling.

## Core Workflow

1. Open with context; reserve full decision for after evidence.
   - On first screen, state research date, price, market cap, valuation and short thesis.
   - Don't front-load full buy/hold/sell table unless user explicitly asks for executive memo.
   - Put detailed recommendation, investor-specific actions and price bands near end, after business quality, risk and valuation have been argued.
   - Separate 'good business' from 'good investment at this price'.
