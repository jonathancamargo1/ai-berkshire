# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

To create an open and welcoming community, we as contributors and maintainers commit to:

Providing a harassment-free experience for everyone participating in our community, regardless of age, physical disability, race, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of constructive behavior include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism in a friendly manner
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Demonstrating empathy with other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Using sexually-charged language or imagery and unwanted sexual attention or advances
- Provocative, insulting, or derogatory comments and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as physical or email address, without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment

## Enforcement

If you witness or are targeted by inappropriate behavior, contact the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
