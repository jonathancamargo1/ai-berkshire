## Tier 1: Exceptional Quality (★★★★★)

Companies with oligopoly/monopoly-level pricing power + exceptional profitability + clear growth path

### 1. Baichushield (688188) | 478B¥ Market Cap
**Core Thesis**: Laser cutting "brain" system, 78% GM / 57% NM - STAR Board profitability king
- Market share >60% in low-power segment; customer price insensitive
- Extreme operational leverage: 2.196B revenue → 1.113B net income
- Risk: Laser equipment growth slowdown, high-power market penetration

### 2. Lanqi Technology (688008) | 3,031B¥ Market Cap
**Core Thesis**: One of three global memory interface chip oligopolists (36.8% share), DDR5 era quantity + price growth
- With Renesas, Rambus split market; extremely high tech barriers
- 62% GM / 39% NM; Fabless light-asset high-return model
- Risk: DDR5 saturation slowdown; valuation already prices in high expectations

### 3. Angie Technology (688019) | 484B¥ Market Cap
**Core Thesis**: CMP polishing fluid domestic substitution leader, ROIC 20%, 40%+ CAGR for 7 years
- 30% domestic share, broken foreign monopoly; 14nm scaling volume production
- 57% GM / 31% NM; semiconductor material "consumables" strong stickiness
- Risk: Downstream fab expansion volatility; advanced node product breakthroughs needed

### 4. Huafeng Test (688200) | 558B¥ Market Cap
**Core Thesis**: Largest domestic semiconductor test system supplier, 40% NM profitability
- Broken foreign monopoly in analog/mixed-signal testing
- SoC test system breakthrough opening larger TAM
- Risk: Semiconductor cyclicality; receivables collection needs monitoring

### 5. Qiang Yi Shares (688809) | 586B¥ Market Cap
**Core Thesis**: MEMS probe card leader, ROE 30% / 65% GM - core consumable for AI chip testing
- Leading domestic probe card share, benefits from AI chip test boom
- Consumables model: test volume ↑ → procurement ↑, deeply bound to customers
- Risk: Top 5 customers >82% concentration, single-customer dependency
