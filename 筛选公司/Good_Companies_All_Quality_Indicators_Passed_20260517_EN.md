3. **All Free Cash Flow Positive** — True cash earnings
4. **Zero Shareholder Dilution** — Most conduct buybacks

This is the financial profile of "first-class good companies": high margins (pricing power) + high ROE (capital efficiency) + strong cash flow (real profits) + no dilution (shareholder respect).
