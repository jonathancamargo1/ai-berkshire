### Financial Characteristics Summary of Buffett's Stock Picking

1. **High ROE is core screening condition**: Passing companies 10-year average ROE commonly 15%+ above, top-tier holdings (Apple, Moody's, AXP) exceed 25%. Only OXY and PetroChina exceptions—both "cheap opportunistic buys" rather than typical Buffett long-term holds.

2. **Free Cash Flow almost universally positive**: Buffett rarely buys companies with negative cash flow. Only exception is bottom-fishing during cycles (e.g., 2020 OXY).

3. **Clear high gross margin preference**: Apple(46%), Coca-Cola(62%), Moody's(74%), VeriSign(88%), American Express(63%). Buffett's favorite companies typically have >40% gross margins, indicating strong pricing power.

4. **Net Margin >10% is norm**: Most core holdings net margin 10-30% range. Kroger(1.8%) and Nucor(5.4%) are rare low net margin exceptions with relatively small positions.

5. **Buyback over dilution**: Nearly all holdings conduct buybacks, 5-year share count not only not diluted but actually reduced. This is Buffett's explicit preferred capital allocation method.

6. **Adequate interest coverage**: Excluding financial companies, all holdings >5x interest coverage, majority >10x. Buffett intensely dislikes debt risk.

7. **Rules of failure/marginal cases**:
   - Kraft Heinz: High-price acquisition + goodwill impairment = Buffett admits "made a mistake"
   - OXY: Special opportunity of "bought when cheap", atypical holding
   - Cyclical industries (energy, steel) naturally difficult to meet stable ROE and net margin requirements

**Core Conclusion**: Buffett's holdings highly concentrated in companies with "high ROE + high gross margin + strong cash flow + low leverage + ongoing buyback". These companies typically have strong moats (brand, network effects, switching costs), enabling sustained excess returns. The 7 screening factors effectively identify his core holding logic.
