## Key Investment Insights

1. **Pricing Power Dominance** - All passing companies maintain 70%+ gross margins, indicating exceptional pricing power
2. **Cash Generation** - Strong FCF across all companies despite demand volatility
3. **ROE Stability** - Consistent 20-34% ROE demonstrates capital efficiency
4. **Moat Strength** - Brand heritage and geographic distribution create durable competitive advantages

**Sector Conclusion**: Food & Beverages is a core defensive holding sector with companies showing exceptional financial quality. Baijiu leaders (Moutai, Wuliangye) merit portfolio prominence.
