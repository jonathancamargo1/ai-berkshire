## 1. China Merchants Bank (600036.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥1.01T | **PE(TTM)**: ~6.3x | **Dividend Yield**: ~5%

### Core Investment Logic

Retail banking benchmark with ROE 13%+, NPL 0.95%, provision 412%—best asset quality. Core strength: low-cost deposits and retail customer management (14T+ AUM), creating persistent net interest margin advantage.

### Moat Analysis

Brand + retail customer base creates deepest moat. Merchants Bank brand dominating retail finance; high-net-worth stickiness high; Gold/Sunflower+ contribute 80%+ AUM; private banking scale #1. Triple-digit billion R&D continuous.

### Valuation Assessment

Cheap. PE 6.3x, PB ~1.0x at historical low. For ROE 13%+, valuation provides ample safety margin. 5% yield provides holding floor.
