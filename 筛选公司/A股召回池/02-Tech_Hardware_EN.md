## 1. Accelink Technologies (300308) — Optical Module Global Leader

### Basic Information

| Metric | Data |
|--------|------|
| Market Cap | ~950-980B¥ (stock price ~880¥) |
| PE(TTM) | ~64x |
| Dividend Yield | 0.16% |

### Business Quality ★★★★

Commercial model relatively clear: supplying ultra-large-scale data centers with high-speed optical modules, essentially "selling shovels" to AI infrastructure. Revenue grows with AI capex, customers are Google, Microsoft, Meta etc cloud giants. 2025 net income ~15B¥ (est.), 2026 institutional forecast 23-26B¥, extremely fast growth.

But note: optical modules remain manufacturing industry essence, technology iteration rapid (400G→800G→1.6T), product lifecycle 2-3 years each generation requiring continuous R&D investment. Not true "one-time investment, perpetual harvest" simple business. Gross margin 30%+ excellent in hardware but far below software/platform companies. Net margin 25%+ competitive.

### Moat Strength ★★★★

- **Technology Leadership**: 800G market share >50%, 1.6T leads competitors 6-12 months, "mass-produce one generation, develop one generation, research one generation" cadence
- **Customer Binding**: Deeply embedded in Google OCS ecosystem, core optical module supplier with high switching cost
- **Scale Effect**: World's largest optical module manufacturer, production scale and yield control cost advantages
- **However**: New Eastech etc competitors rapidly catching up, optical module industry history shows leader transitions (Finisar acquired by II-VI), technology barriers not insurmountable

### Management Quality ★★★★

Controlling shareholder Wang Weixiu (enjoys State Council special allowance), voluntarily stepped down after 2016 Suzhou Xuangui acquisition, management handed to founder Liu Sheng (Chinese Academy Sciences Master's + Georgia Tech PhD). Forms "technology expert + professional manager" governance structure. Management excels strategically — 2016 Xuangui acquisition decisive step, recent CPO, silicon photonics frontier technology continuous layout.

### Valuation Attractiveness ★★

Current PE(TTM) ~64x, 2026 forward PE ~40-42x. Given 120%-150% profit growth, PEG ~0.3-0.5, appears "not expensive." But problem: AI capex cycle risk, optical module industry historical cyclicality (2018-2019 major decline), current trillion RMB market cap whether fully reflects optimistic expectations. Market gives extremely high growth certainty premium.

### Overall Score ★★★★

### One-Line Summary

AI optical module sector absolute leader, technology leadership+customer binding create strong moat, trillion market cap already hides high growth expectations, worth watching after industry panic or downward forecast revision.

## 2. Cambricon Technologies (688012) — Etching Equipment

### Basic Information

| Metric | Data |
|--------|------|
| Market Cap | ~200-210B¥ (stock price ~330¥) |
| PE(TTM) | ~93x (static), 2026E ~64x |
| Dividend Yield | 0.09% |

### Business Quality ★★★★

Semiconductor equipment is typical "selling shovels to gold miners" model. Once customer validates equipment, replacement cost extremely high (process revalidation takes months to year). Etching equipment is one of three core chip manufacturing steps, large market space, extreme tech barriers. 2025 revenue ~12.4B¥ (yoy+37%), net profit ~2.1B¥ (yoy+31%), solid growth.

But note: semiconductor equipment industry has clear cyclicality driven by downstream wafer fab capex. Cambricon globally faces Lam Research, Tokyo Electron giant competition; domestic market generates vast majority of revenue.

### Moat Strength ★★★★

- **Technology Barriers**: Etching equipment R&D 5-7 year cycle, Cambricon already cracked 5nm and below advanced process, China's only etching equipment merchant cracking international first-tier customer list
- **Customer Stickiness**: Long equipment validation cycle, high replacement cost once on-line
- **Patent Moat**: Accumulated core patents, intentional design differentiation from international competitors avoiding infringement while building own barriers
- **However**: Globally, Lam and Tokyo Electron control etching market 70%+ share; Cambricon growth highly dependent on domestic replacement policy push and geopolitical environment

### Management Quality ★★★★★

Founder Dr. Yin Zhiyao (returned to start company at 60 after 13 years VP at Applied Materials), industry-top technology expert. Management team Duy Zhiyou, Ni Tuqiang etc all semiconductor equipment veterans. Company implements all-staff equity incentives, core team stable. Yin proposed "5-10 years covering 50-60% of IC critical domain equipment" ambitious goal, showing strategic vision and execution.

### Valuation Attractiveness ★★

PE(TTM) ~93x, even by 2026 consensus forecast net profit 30.75B¥, forward PE still 64x. Semiconductor equipment recognized good sector, market already priced extreme valuation. Compare global leader Lam Research PE ~25x, Cambricon valuation premium from "domestic replacement" logic and higher growth expectations. If replacement pace slows or sector cycles down, valuation faces material pressure.

### Overall Score ★★★★

### One-Line Summary

Domestic etching equipment absolute leader, management is scarce top-tier technology startup team, current valuation fully prices domestic replacement optimism, needs better price window wait.

## 3. Landeng Technology (688008) — Memory Interface Chips

### Basic Information

| Metric | Data |
|--------|------|
| Market Cap | ~260B¥ (stock price ~248¥) |
| PE(TTM) | ~118x (based EPS 2.11¥) |
| Dividend Yield | 0.28% |

### Business Quality ★★★★★

Memory interface chips are mandatory server RAM module components; each DDR5 RAM stick needs 1 RCD chip plus multiple data buffer chips. Globally only Landeng and Renesas two suppliers, oligopoly monopoly. Gross margin 60%+, net margin 35%, near chip design company optimal level. Business model simple: design chips→outsource production→sell to memory and server makers.

This is true "good business": demand grows with server shipments and DDR5 penetration, market extreme concentration, product standardized. 2026Q1 net profit 0.847B¥ (yoy+61%), full-year forecast ~3B¥.

### Moat Strength ★★★★★

- **Dual-Oligopoly Monopoly**: Globally only Landeng and Renesas provide complete DDR5 memory interface solution, barriers extreme
- **Standard Setter**: Participates JEDEC standard-setting, important DDR5 standard contributor
- **Certification Barriers**: Products require Intel, AMD platform certification, new entrants face years-long certification cycle
- **Technology Iteration Advantage**: DDR4 to DDR5 successful transition proves technology continuity
- **However**: If future DDR6 standard changes alter technology routes, or if Intel/AMD self-develop interface chips, monopoly pattern may break

### Management Quality ★★★★

Founder Dr. Yang Chonghe, successful serial entrepreneur (1997 first startup success acquired by IDT $85M), 2004 founded Landeng. Deep semiconductor industry accumulation. Management philosophy clear: "Predict market trends, assemble strong team, keep sufficient capital." R&D staff 71%, R&D investment ~15% (exceeds peers).

But note: Yang Chonghe holds US citizenship (China permanent residency), whether geopolitical risk exists worth attention currently.

### Valuation Attractiveness ★★

Current PE(TTM) ~118x, 2026 expected net profit 3B¥ calculate forward PE ~87x. Even conservative 2027 forecast 3.8B¥, forward PE still 68x. Oligopoly good business deserves premium, but current means even 2-year profit doubling still >50x PE. Market priced extreme long-term growth expectations.

### Overall Score ★★★★

### One-Line Summary

Global memory interface chip dual-oligopoly, business quality among six best (high gross margin+oligopoly+standard-setting), valuation high-flying, suit long-term tracking, buy panic moments aggressively.

## 4. New Eastech (300502) — High-Speed Optical Modules

### Basic Information

| Metric | Data |
|--------|------|
| Market Cap | ~500B¥ (stock price ~564¥) |
| PE(TTM) | ~40x (based 2025 profit ~94-98B¥) |
| Dividend Yield | ~0.1% (est.) |

### Business Quality ★★★☆

Same sector as Accelink, similar commercial model — supply high-speed optical modules to data centers. Difference: Eastech focuses mid-short-distance data center market, pursues LPO (Linear-drive Photonic Module) tech path, emphasizes cost control and vertical integration (optical chip fabrication→packaging→module full coverage).

2025 revenue ~26.9B¥, net profit ~94-98B¥. 2026 institutional forecast net profit 168-178B¥ (yoy+70-80%), extremely fast growth. Gross margin 38%, net margin 30%+, performance excellent in optical module industry.

But note: Compared Accelink's deep customer binding, Eastech customer concentration and structure disclosure sparse, possibly weaker voice in 1.6T premium market vs Accelink.

### Moat Strength ★★★

- **Vertical Integration**: Optical chip→module full product chain coverage, clear cost advantage
- **Technology Differentiation**: LPO path has functional/cost advantages, suits short-distance applications
- **Production Capacity Scale**: Strong rapid capacity expansion ability
- **However**: Optical module industry fierce competition, Eastech higher-end market (1.6T, CPO) layout shallower than Accelink; founder once violated regulations reduced holdings punished, core tech staff legal disputes, long-term trust construction concerns

### Management Quality ★★★

Chairman Gao Guangrong (secondary education, 1998 started business, 2008 co-founded Eastech). 2023 securities law violation excessive stock reduction fined 314.9M¥ (family trust hidden shareholding+limit breach), clear integrity mark-down. GM Huang Xiaomin tech-origin expert (Southeast University+Electronic Science University Master's), early copyright infringement criminal conviction.

Management tech execution not bad (10G to 800G multi-generation iteration showing), but credibility records flawed. Duan Yongping standard "not do business with untrustworthy people," warning signal.

### Valuation Attractiveness ★★★

PE(TTM) ~40x, 2026 forward PE ~29x calculated by forecast 170B¥ profit. Optical module three brothers lowest valuation, growth not weak, appears relative valuation attraction. But consider management credibility risk discount and industry cyclicality.

### Overall Score ★★★

### One-Line Summary

Optical module second-seed, vertical integration cost advantage, valuation relatively fair, but management credibility flaws critical shortcoming, violates "buying stock buys people" core principle.

## 5. Reflex Communications (300394) — Optical Passive Devices

### Basic Information

| Metric | Data |
|--------|------|
| Market Cap | ~310B¥ (stock price ~400¥) |
| PE(TTM) | ~110-115x (based recent four quarters ~2.17B¥) |
| Dividend Yield | 0.42% |

### Business Quality ★★★★★

Reflex business model is optical communications "selling parts" business: supplies ultra-precision optical passive devices and optical engine modules, upstream of optical module makers. Gross margin 52%, net margin 38% — extremely rare in hardware manufacturing, near chip design company levels. Essentially leveraging ultra-precision manufacturing technology building barriers as "hidden champion."

2025 revenue 5.163B¥ (yoy+59%), net profit 2.017B¥ (yoy+50%). 2026Q1 net profit 0.492B¥ (yoy+46%), growth momentum remains strong. By 2025 external revenue, company holds 11.7% global optical device supplier share ranking first.

Client structure worth attention: 2024 Fabrinet alone contributed 61.69% revenue, extreme customer concentration, both proving core competitiveness (global top-tier precision maker chooses Reflex), also risk point.

### Moat Strength ★★★★

- **Ultra-Precision Manufacturing Craft**: Deep accumulation in precision ceramics, engineering plastics, composite metal, optical glass tech, hard for competitors short-term replicate
- **Global #1 Market Share**: 11.7% global optical device market share #1
- **Customer Certification Barriers**: Once on-boarding top-tier customer supply chain, replacement cost high
- **However**: Customer concentration too high (single customer 60%+) obvious risk; if Fabrinet transfers orders or disperses suppliers, performance suffers major shock

### Management Quality ★★★★

Founders Zou Zhinong (board chair+chief engineer) and Ouyang (GM) couple co-started 2005. 20 years focused optical communication precision devices, showing "focus doing one thing well" operation philosophy. Company consecutive seven years named "China optical communication most competitive enterprise 10 strong" #1. Management stable, focused, low-profile, fits value investor excellent management expectations.

2026 launches HK listing, showing management capital operations proactive planning.

### Valuation Attractiveness ★

PE(TTM) reaches 110-115x, history valuation 94th percentile high. Even 2026 expected net profit 32-33B¥ calculate forward PE ~95x. Even conservative 2027 forecast 4.1B¥, forward PE still 75x.

Business indeed good, current price fully cashed-out future two-year growth. Market error tolerance extremely low — any below-forecast quarter report triggers major stock pullback.

### Overall Score ★★★☆

### One-Line Summary

Optical communications "precision manufacturing hidden champion," business quality excellent (high margin+global #1), but 115x valuation eliminates safety edge, patience waits sector pullback or earnings major release post reasonable price.

## 6. Foxconn Industrial Internet (601138) — AI Servers (Exemption C Passed)

### Basic Information

| Metric | Data |
|--------|------|
| Market Cap | ~1.05T¥ (stock price ~64¥) |
| PE(TTM) | ~30x |
| Dividend Yield | 1.93% |

### Business Quality ★★★

Foxconn commercial model essence is AI server ODM/OEM outsourcing. Global AI server market share >40%, Nvidia GB-series and Microsoft Azure core outsourcing partner. 2026Q1 revenue 251.1B¥ (yoy+57%), net profit 10.6B¥ (yoy+103%), astounding growth.

But critical weakness: gross margin 7%, net margin 3.8% (also why elimination screening needs "exemption C" pass). Typical "large not fat" business — trillion-level revenue but ultra-thin profit margin. Each server earns little, surviving via massive output. If downstream demand fluctuates or customers pressure pricing, profit elasticity limited.

### Moat Strength ★★★

- **Scale Barrier**: Annual capacity >2M AI servers, globally unmatched
- **Customer Deep Binding**: Joint R&D cutting in early design stage, top five customers contribute >60% revenue
- **Global Layout**: Multi-nation production bases dispersing geopolitical risk
- **Yield Control**: Large-scale mass production yield control core competitiveness
- **However**: Outsourcing model nature lacks pricing power, core profit captured by brands (Nvidia, cloud makers); 7% gross margin means almost no bargaining capacity; if competitor (Wistron, Quanta) capacity expands, pressure margin further

### Management Quality ★★★

Supported by Foxconn/HTC system with global manufacturing experience. As Foxconn group A-share platform, independence questioned — major decisions possibly more serve group interests vs A-share minority shareholders.

### Valuation Attractiveness ★★★★

PE(TTM) ~30x, 2026 expected net profit 51.5B¥ calculate forward PE ~21x. For trillion-revenue, 45%+ profit growth company, 21x forward PE seems reasonable even slightly low.

But low valuation has low valuation reason: 3.8% net margin means outsourcing essence unchanged, long-term ROE ceiling limited.

### Overall Score ★★★

### One-Line Summary

Global AI server outsourcing absolute size king, valuation reasonable with dividend, but 7% gross margin outsourcing nature decides "earning hard money," not meeting "good business" highest standard.
