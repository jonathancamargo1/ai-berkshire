## Key Findings

1. **Equipment > Fabless** - Semiconductor equipment companies (70%+ margins) outperformed fabless chip designers
2. **FCF Sensitivity** - 50% failure rate driven by negative or marginal free cash flow
3. **Margin Quality** - Passing companies show >30% gross margins, indicating strong pricing power
4. **Cyclicality** - Tech hardware shows higher cyclical vulnerability than consumer-facing sectors

**Sector Conclusion**: Technology Hardware has high-quality leaders (optical, etching equipment) but broad sector weakness in marginal players. Focus on equipment suppliers with 60%+ margins and positive FCF.
