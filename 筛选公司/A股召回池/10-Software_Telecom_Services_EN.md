## 1. Kingsoft Office (688111.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥115B | **PE(TTM)**: ~50x

### Core Investment Logic

Domestic office software absolute leader with 6.72B MAU creating data flywheel. AI transformation accelerating (WPS AI 8M MAU, +307% YoY). Policy-driven government-enterprise replacement. Transition from "selling license" to "selling AI services" business model upgrade materializing.

### Moat Analysis

Multi-layer moats—user scale/habit barriers (6.72B MAU), policy barriers on government (almost irreplaceable), 30-year doc tech switching cost, AI-era data moats.

### Valuation Assessment

Expensive. Even considering AI high-growth expectations, 50x PE needs long digestion unless AI paid-user rate rises fast.

## 2. China Mobile (600941.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥2.0T | **PE(TTM)**: ~15x | **Dividend Yield**: ~4-5%

### Core Investment Logic

Largest telecom operator with quasi-monopoly (10B+ users) and infrastructure. Transition from "pipe" to "telecom + tech"; government-enterprise and AI compute as second curves. High dividend + cheap valuation = steady returns.

### Moat Analysis

Ultra-wide moats—license monopoly + infrastructure scale + 10B user base = three barriers. Telecom industry three-oligarch stable format; price war unlikely. 5G/compute network massive investment further raises entry barrier.

### Valuation Assessment

Cheap. 15x PE on global telecom leader is fair-to-low, 4-5% yield extremely attractive.

## 5. Fengsheng Media (002027.SZ)

**Market Cap**: ~¥88-107B | **PE(TTM)**: ~13-17x

### Core Investment Logic

Elevator media absolute leader (64% share), "high reach + frequency + completion" forced exposure scene irreplaceable. Light-asset ops bring ultra-high ROE (30%+), excellent cash. Advertising recovery has clear elasticity.

### Moat Analysis

First-mover + scale build moat—quality downtown locations limited and mostly locked by Fengsheng (long contracts); newcomers hard to match network. But single business, earnings cycle-dependent. Business structure vulnerable to macro cycle.

### Valuation Assessment

Cheap-to-fair. 13-17x PE on light-asset high-ROE model obvious low.
