## 1. Wanhua Chemical (600309.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥280B | **PE(2026E)**: ~9x | **Dividend Yield**: ~3%

### Core Investment Logic

Global MDI leader with ~30% market share. Global only 5 MDI suppliers (Wanhua, BASF, Covestro, Huntsman, Dow), oligarch format ultra-stable. 2026 capacity release year (Penglai, Meishan bases), ¥18.4B profit (+28%). Wanhua competitive strength: continuous tech breakthroughs and cost control—lowest per-ton MDI cost globally.

### Moat Analysis

Tech + capital + environment triple barriers ultra-strong. Tech: MDI synthesis ultra-complex (phosgene reaction, safety extreme), globally only 5 companies control; Wanhua sole China company mastering via self-R&D. Capital: new MDI unit ¥5-10B investment, 3-5 year cycle. Environment: phosgene deadly, new capacity environmental approval nearly impossible. But MDI downstream (fridge insulation, autos, building) growth limited (5-7%); high growth more from new materials (POE, PMMA, aldehydes), competition not yet solidified.

### Valuation Assessment

Cheap. PE(2026E) 9x for ROE 15%+, growth 20%+, global oligarch position extremely attractive. Historical PE center 15-20x, current discount significant. Market worries chem downcycle and real estate demand; Wanhua weakening cyclicality through diversification.

## 2. Hualu Hengsheng (600426.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥70B | **PE(2026E)**: ~9-10x | **Dividend Yield**: ~3.5%

### Core Investment Logic

Coal chemicals "low-cost king." Core strength: flexible production platform—same unit flexibly switches between urea, DMF, adipic, acetic, always producing highest-margin product. 2026 ¥6.8B profit (+17%). Jingzhou base startup opens new growth.

### Moat Analysis

Cost advantage + flexible production build differentiation. Hualu's gasification efficiency industry-leading, per-unit energy <15-20% below peers, multi-year process optimization. Flexible co-production makes profit in any chem product price combo; anti-cycle far better than single-product. But coal chemicals whole sector faces carbon-neutral constraints; long-term need green chem transition. Main products (urea, DMF, adipic) barriers not as high as Wanhua MDI.

### Valuation Assessment

Cheap. PE 9-10x on ROE 15%+ and stable dividend. Coal chemicals low-cost advantage makes profits even at cycle bottom (never lost money historically), certainty deserves premium but market not given.

## 3. North New Materials (000786.SZ)

**Market Cap**: ~¥47B | **PE(TTM)**: ~14.5x | **ROE**: ~20%+

### Core Investment Logic

Gypsum board absolute leader, >60% domestic market share. Only 5 suppliers (North New #1, Taishan #2 under China Building Materials, both under same parent) = quasi-monopoly. Low product price (¥/㎡) but transport radius limits (~300km) naturally create region monopoly. ROE constantly 20%+, cash flow excellent.

### Moat Analysis

Scale + transport radius ultra-strong. North New deployed 100+ production lines nationally, each covers 300km radius; new entrants even with tech cannot profitably compete in existing line regions (transport eats profit). Brand "Dragon" high recognition in renovation. But gypsum demand highly real-estate related (new renovation + stock renewal); real estate persistent downturn faces demand pressure. Company pursuing second curve (waterproof, coatings, etc), effect TBD.

### Valuation Assessment

Fair-to-low. PE 14.5x on 20%+ ROE and 60%+ market share monopoly position attractive. Historical PE center 18-22x, current ~30% discount. Real estate bottom will bring valuation repair catalyst.

## 4. China Fiberglass (600176.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥110B | **PE(2026E)**: ~16-17x

### Core Investment Logic

Global fiberglass leader, highest capacity globally. Fiberglass formed China Giants, Taishan Glass, Chongqing International "three giants" oligarch (CR3 >70%). Downstream benefits wind blades, auto lightweight, AI server PCB high-growth. Fiberglass price 2025 bottom touched, 2026 in up-cycle.

### Moat Analysis

Scale + tech + capital build barriers. Fiberglass kiln huge investment (¥1B+ per line), long build cycle (2y+), tech high (kiln design, glass formula core know-how). China Giants in E9 ultra-fine yarn, high-modulus tech leading. Oligarch format means price floor. But fiberglass obvious cyclicality; capacity/demand waves create price volatility; up 90% ytd, short-term profit-taking risk large. Good company but valuation short-term expensive.

### Valuation Assessment

Fair but near-term pricey. PE(2026E) 16-17x on cycle-up period profit; if 2027 oversupply/margin fall, actual PE will balloon. Up 90% ytd means near-term hot, pullback risk large.

**Market Cap**: ~¥105B | **PE(2026E)**: ~10-14x

Global vitamin leader (VA top 3, VE #2 globally), extending to new materials (PPS, PPA engineering plastics). Vitamin oligarch stable (global only 5-6 main suppliers), supply concentrated. 2026 vitamin prices in up-cycle (DBS exit, BASF accident), new materials ramping, profit expectations optimistic.

Tech + environment + oligarch format build barriers. Vitamin synthesis complex (VA 20+ steps), new entrant 5-10 year R&D. Environment approval strict limiting new capacity. Global oligarch format (VA only DBS, BASF, Huasheng, Adisseo, etc) decades stable. New materials (PPS resin) breaking Japan Toray, DIC monopoly. But vitamin price strong cyclicality (history 3-5x swings), Huasheng profit volatile; new materials in early ramp, success replicating vitamin TBD.

Cheap. PE 10-14x (depends vitamin price assumption) on global oligarch position and new materials second curve, valuation attractive. Management (founder family Hu Baipan) low-key pragmatic, long-term R&D investment, classic "hidden champion" style.
