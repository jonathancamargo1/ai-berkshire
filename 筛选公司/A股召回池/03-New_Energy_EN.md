## 1. CATL (300750)

**Market Cap**: 2.1T¥ | **PE(TTM)**: 25x

### Core Investment Thesis

Global power battery leader with 37%+ market share. Scale efficiency and technology iteration speed far exceed competitors. 2025 net profit 72.2B¥ (+42%), energy storage business becoming second growth curve opening ceiling.

### Moat Analysis

Technology moat extremely deep: Kirin battery, Shen Xing hypercharging, condensed battery continuously lead industry. Scale moat: global largest production capacity with cost advantage. Customer lock-in: bound to Tesla, BMW, Mercedes etc global mainstream automakers with high switching cost. Brand effect provides premium in energy storage tenders. Risk: equipment growth deceleration, high-power market penetration.

### Valuation Assessment

25x PE applies to 42% profit growth with PEG<1 seemingly reasonable. But 21T market cap means future growth must decelerate; needs assessment of long-term stable profit midpoint.

## 2. BYD (002594)

**Market Cap**: 1T¥ | **PE(TTM)**: 23x

### Core Investment Thesis

Global EV sales champion (4.27M units), vertical integration model (battery+chip+vehicle) builds extreme cost control power. Global expansion accelerating with ex-China revenue 38.7%, replicating China success path globally.

### Moat Analysis

Vertical integration core barrier: mine to battery to vehicle full supply chain self-R&D self-production, cost control ability none can match. Scale+tech iteration speed makes competitors struggle to catch up. Brand mix low-to-high (Yuan Shang, Teng Shi), product matrix covers 50k-100k price band. But auto industry naturally fierce competition; moat width not like consumer goods.

### Valuation Assessment

23x PE applied to high growth reasonable, but auto industry price wars ongoing, whether net profit rate sustains core variable. If maintaining 5%+ net margin, current valuation attractive.
