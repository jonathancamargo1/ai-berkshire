**Market Cap**: ¥550 billion | **PE(TTM)**: 12x | **Dividend Yield**: 5.6%

Global appliance leader with diversified portfolio (appliances + industrial automation + building tech + robotics) entering mature harvest stage. 12x PE + 5.6% yield = A-shares' most cost-effective blue chip. Textbook case of good company at good price.

### Moat Analysis

Three-layer moat stacking: brand + channel + scale. Full appliance category coverage with strongest online/offline channels. 40%+ overseas revenue provides globalization breadth. Management professionalism among A-shares' highest; Fang Hongbo's team shows exceptional execution. T+3 sales-production model and digital capability build efficiency moat.

### Valuation Assessment

12x PE significantly undervalued; even at zero growth deserves 15-18x. 5.6% yield provides safety floor with minimal downside.
