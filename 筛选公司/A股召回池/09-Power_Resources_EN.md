## 1. China Yangtze Power (600900.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥640B | **PE(TTM)**: ~19.5x | **Dividend Yield**: ~3.6%

### Core Investment Logic

Exclusive control of Yangtze mainstream six world-class cascade hydropower stations (Three Gorges, Gezhouba, Xiluodu, Xiangjiaba, Wudongde, Baihetan). "One drop generates six times" six-reservoir coordination creates certainty cash flow. Low-rate environment + high dividend (promise >70%) + depreciation release (Three Gorges machines reaching amortization) create long-term profit growth certainty.

### Moat Analysis

Supply-side resource monopoly is ultimate moat—Yangtze mainstream hydro resources non-renewable, development rights granted by state, fully concentrated. Six-reservoir coordination synergy generates 60-70B additional kWh yearly. Policy barriers extreme; no new entrant possible.

### Valuation Assessment

Fair. PE 19.5x at 3-year historical low, far below power industry average 29.7x. Yield 3.6% in low-rate environment extremely attractive.

## 2. China Shenhua (601088.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥1.0T | **PE(TTM)**: ~15-16x | **Dividend Yield**: ~6%

### Core Investment Logic

Coal-power-transport integrated chain creates internal synergy, anti-cycle far exceeds pure-coal. 2025 completed ¥100B asset restructure; resource reserves and profit further enriched. High yield (~6%) + cheap valuation create strong safety margin.

### Moat Analysis

Integrated chain (mines + power plants + railways + ports + shipping) creates system barriers single-environment competitors unable replicate. Domestic #1 coal reserves; self-owned transport network cuts cost and ensures supply stability.

### Valuation Assessment

Cheap-to-fair. 2026 earnings forecast PE only 7-8x plus 6% yield = ample safety margin.

## 3. Zijin Mining (601899.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥1.0T | **PE(TTM)**: ~14-15x

### Core Investment Logic

Global mining expansion execution excellent, gold-copper dual mainline benefit from global inflation and energy transition. Volume sustained high growth (2025-2027 copper-gold CAGR 15%+), transformed from "cycle stock" to "resource + growth" dual-engine.

### Moat Analysis

Moat relatively limited—mining essentially resource acquisition + operational efficiency comparison. Zijin advantage in strong management project acquisition and mine operations (low-grade ore development tech leading), lacks monopoly resource barrier. Copper-gold price still core variable.

### Valuation Assessment

Fair. 14-15x PE on high-growth stage fair-to-low, but consider mining cyclicality and precious metal price volatility risk.

## 4. Shaanxi Coal (601225.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥220B | **PE(TTM)**: ~13x | **Dividend Yield**: ~5%

### Core Investment Logic

Shaanbei high-quality coal mine resources (per-ton cost lowest), stable high dividend (yield ~5%), coal prices mid-point though declining, but profit resilience strong. 2026 profit recovery expected (¥19.6B, +17%).

Resource quality advantage—Shaanbei coal thick seams, shallow burial, low extraction cost. But moat width not equal to Shenhua's integrated model; essentially single coal extraction enterprise, commodity cycle impact large.
