## 1. Sany Heavy Industry (600031.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥187B | **PE(TTM)**: ~18-22x | **PB**: ~2.4x

### Core Investment Logic

Global construction machinery leader with #1 excavator market share. Overseas >50%, true transition from "China leader" to "global leader." Digital transformation (tree root IIoT platform) + globalization dual-engine. Machinery at cycle bottom, domestic utilization rising, overseas ramping.

### Moat Analysis

Brand + channel + service network build global moat. Complete global dealer network and service coverage; customer lock-in from after-sales and spare parts. Digital capability (remote diagnosis, predictive maintenance) deepens stickiness. Scale efficiency advantages. But cyclicality evident; Caterpillar/Komatsu still have high-end advantages.

### Valuation Assessment

Cycle-bottom valuation reasonable-to-low. PE 18-22x seems not low but at cycle bottom (profit at trough), normalized PE probably 12-15x. PB 2.4x vs 15%+ normalized ROE attractive.

## 2. CAAC Optoelectronic (002179.SZ)

**Market Cap**: ~¥78.5B | **PE(TTM)**: ~28-35x

### Core Investment Logic

Military connector absolute leader with 40%+ defense market share. Connectors are weapons "nervous system"; each equipment upgrade needs higher-density, higher-reliability. Tech + certification barriers extremely high; 5-8 year new entrant cert timeline. Civil (telecom, EVs) opening second curve.

### Moat Analysis

Dual barriers ultra-strong. Tech: high-reliability connectors need deep material/process/design accumulation, failure costs extreme. Cert: military suppliers need strict security/quality approval; cycle long; once loaded very difficult to replace. But defense budget constraints limit growth ceiling; 2023-2025 order adjustment period shows volatility.

### Valuation Assessment

Expensive but wait-able. PE 28-35x at historical mid-high, on depressed profit (order adjustment). If 2026-2027 military orders warm and profit restores, forward PE maybe 20-25x. Good sector, good company, need better price.

## 3. Ziguang Guomicro (002049.SZ)

**Market Cap**: ~¥59B | **PE(2026E)**: ~38x

### Core Investment Logic

Specialty chip leader (military FPGA, security chip) with ultra-high certification barrier. Market entry extreme (security cert + military cert + tech accumulation), once verified almost no replacement. Margins 60%+, net 40%+, profitability top-tier.

### Moat Analysis

Cert + tech barriers ultra-strong. Specialty FPGA domestically 2-3 suppliers only; Ziguang leading. Security chip (SIM/bank/ID chip) top 3 globally. But earnings volatile—military orders show "big year/small year" pattern; 2024-2025 low point, profit crashed. Specialty chip market total size limited (hundred billion level), ceiling exists.

### Valuation Assessment

Expensive. PE(2026E) 38x on profit low-point recovery expectations. If recovery misses, valuation pressured. Given volatility and growth ceiling, insufficient safety margin.

## 4. Green Harmonic Drive (688017.SH)

**Market Cap**: ~¥39B | **PE(TTM)**: ~429x

### Core Investment Logic

Domestic harmonic reducer leader breaking Japan Hara-Moto monopoly. Core robot component (10-15% of robot cost), high barriers, huge localization space. Humanoid robot narrative provides valuation imagination.

### Moat Analysis

Tech barrier exists but not insurmountable. Green Harmonic performance nearing Hara-Moto, cost advantages. But Geleor, Biaxial and other domestic competitors catching fast; not "winner-takes-all." Traditional industrial robot slowdown; humanoid large-scale production 3-5 years away, near-term profit not enough to support.

### Valuation Assessment

Severely overvalued. PE 429x means current price driven entirely by humanoid vision, not fundamentals. Even on optimistic 2027 profit, PE 80-100x. "Dream pricing" not "value pricing." No safety margin for value investor.
